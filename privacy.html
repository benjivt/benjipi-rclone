Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

"benjipi rclone" is a personal Google OAuth client. Its only user is its owner, Benjamin Adjepong, who uses it with rclone to store encrypted backups of his home server in his own Google Drive.

What data it accesses

It requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, which gives it access to just the files and folders it creates. It cannot see, read, or change any other files in Google Drive.

How the data is used

The files it creates are the owner's own backups. They are encrypted before upload. The app has no server: rclone runs on the owner's own machine and talks directly to Google Drive.

Sharing

No data is collected by, sent to, sold to, or shared with anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
